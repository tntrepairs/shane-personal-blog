The Human Resources Manager

Human Resources Department

RE: APPLICATION FOR INDUSTRIAL ATTACHMENT IN DATA ANALYTICS / BUSINESS TECHNOLOGY

Dear Human Resources Manager,

I am writing to apply for an industrial attachment opportunity at your organisation, preferably in a role involving data analysis, business intelligence, reporting or business technology. I am currently studying towards a Bachelor of Science in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics at Botswana Accountancy College. I would value the opportunity to apply what I am learning in a working environment and contribute to the organisation's day-to-day operations.

Between January and April 2026, I worked as a Legal Intern and Debt Collections Assistant in the Collections Department at Precious and Partners Legal Practice. My responsibilities included maintaining customer and account records, documenting calls and promises to pay, updating Excel trackers and assisting with daily, weekly and monthly performance reports. I also worked on spreadsheet automation and dashboard development to make operational information easier to organise and review.

This experience showed me why accurate data matters in practice. A missed follow-up, an incomplete record or an incorrectly updated tracker can affect the next person who relies on that information. It taught me to pay attention to detail, manage competing tasks and communicate clearly with colleagues and customers.

At BAC, my studies in Statistics and Data Analysis, Computer Technology and Fundamentals of Business Finance are strengthening my analytical and technical foundation. Outside my coursework, I have explored digital product and reporting projects, which have encouraged me to think about how technology can simplify business processes. I also served as Assistant Head Boy and a member of the SRC Executive at St. Joseph's College in 2025, where I developed confidence in leadership, collaboration and public speaking.

I am particularly interested in an attachment where I can help with data collection, spreadsheet reporting, documentation, dashboard preparation or process improvement while learning from experienced professionals. I am willing to take on routine responsibilities, ask questions when necessary and build my skills through practical work.

Thank you for considering my application. My curriculum vitae is attached for your review. I would welcome an opportunity to discuss how I could contribute to your organisation during an attachment.

Yours faithfully,

Katso Shane Galetshetse
