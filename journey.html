Learning Through Study

Building habits that support understanding and application.

Finding a rhythm

Adjusting to tertiary study means taking more ownership of how I plan and learn. I have been working on managing study alongside personal projects and setting aside focused time for each.

Learning with intention

Using the library and other focused environments helps me concentrate. I am developing independent study habits and getting better at understanding concepts well enough to apply them.

Improving through reflection

Mistakes are part of learning. Reviewing what did not work helps me adjust my approach, strengthen time management and keep building confidence with technical ideas.

02

Group Work & Leadership

A useful lens for thinking about how teams develop.

Forming

Agreeing on the task, roles and shared direction.

Storming

Working through different perspectives and approaches.

Norming

Building coordination and clearer working habits.

Performing

Applying strengths toward the group’s shared work.

Adjourning

Completing the work and reflecting on what the team learned.

In group work, I have learned to help coordinate tasks and communicate about progress. Tuckman’s stages offer a framework for reflecting on team development; teams may move through these stages differently, and I do not treat them as a fixed script for every group.
